Privacy Policy

family-pipeline is a personal tool used only by its owner.

It uses the YouTube scope youtube. The app only uploads videos the owner made to the owner's own channel as private videos, and adds them to the owner's own playlists. It does not read, share, or sell any other data, and no data is transmitted to third parties.

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
